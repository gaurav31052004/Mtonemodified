Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2025

1. Introduction

MT One provides digital services and software solutions. As our products are primarily digital in nature, our shipping and delivery policy focuses on the delivery of digital access, software licenses, and service activations.

2. Digital Delivery

  • MT One - App & CRM is a mobile application available for download from Google Play Store
  • Upon successful payment, customers will receive immediate access to download and install the application
  • Digital products and services are delivered electronically via email and app store access
  • No physical shipping is required for our core services

3. Service Activation

  • Account activation is processed immediately after successful payment verification
  • Customers will receive login credentials via email within 5-10 minutes of payment confirmation
  • For subscription services, access is granted upon completion of the billing cycle
  • Trial accounts are activated instantly upon registration

4. Delivery Timeline

Free Trial

  • Immediate activation
  • Email confirmation within 5 minutes
  • Full access to trial features

Paid Services

  • Activation within 10 minutes
  • Email delivery of credentials
  • Full service access upon payment

5. Technical Requirements

To ensure smooth delivery and optimal performance, please ensure your device meets the following requirements:

  • Operating System: Android 8.0 or higher, iOS 12.0 or higher
  • Storage: Minimum 100MB free space
  • Internet: Stable internet connection for app download and data synchronization
  • Device: Smartphone or tablet with modern web browser

6. Download & Installation

  • App download is available through Google Play Store and Apple App Store
  • Installation guides are provided via email after purchase
  • Technical support is available for installation assistance
  • Offline functionality is available for basic features once installed

7. International Delivery

MT One services are currently available for users in India. International users may experience different delivery times based on their location and local app store availability.

8. Failed Delivery

  • If you don't receive your access credentials within the specified timeframe, please check your spam/junk folder
  • Contact our support team at info@mtone.in for assistance
  • Include your order/transaction ID for faster resolution
  • We will resend credentials or provide alternative access methods
